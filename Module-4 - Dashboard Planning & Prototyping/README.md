# Module 4 – Dashboard Planning and Prototyping


## Project Overview

This module focuses on planning and developing an interactive Military Power Analytics Dashboard. The dashboard presents complex military data in a clear and visually meaningful format, allowing users to compare the defence capabilities of different countries.

## Objectives

- Design a clear and user-friendly dashboard layout.
- Present important military indicators through suitable visuals.
- Enable country-wise comparison of military power.
- Highlight defence budget, personnel, equipment and global ranking.
- Convert the processed dataset into useful visual insights.

## Dashboard Sections

### 1. Quick Statistics
Displays important summary indicators such as total countries, defence budget, active personnel and military assets.

### 2. Military Power Ranking
Shows the global military power ranking of different countries based on the Power Index.

### 3. Defence Budget Analysis
Compares the defence expenditure of countries and identifies the countries with the highest military spending.

### 4. Personnel Comparison
Presents the comparison of active military personnel across different countries.

### 5. Military Assets Analysis
Compares major military assets such as aircraft, tanks, naval assets and other defence equipment.

### 6. Country Comparison
Allows users to analyse and compare the military strength of selected countries using multiple indicators.
